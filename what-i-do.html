What I Do

I design and direct AI tools for freight work.

I design and direct AI tools for freight work: I map the workflow as it actually runs, decide what the tool may and may not do, set the rules it works under, and review what comes out. The building is done with AI assistance, and I say so wherever the work is shown. My ground is the work itself — air export, receiving, screening, planning — and the tools are shaped by the desks they are meant to sit on. The pattern across all three projects is the same: machines draft, compute, and cross-check; people judge and sign.
