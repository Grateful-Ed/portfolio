TMS Consolidation Agent

Freight consolidation is a judgment call made under time pressure. This agent makes the plan — and prices where the plan strains.

The TMS Consolidation Agent works a small sample world — a freight forwarder, an anchor retail client, eight shipments across three lanes — and produces two things: a consolidation plan, and a priced struggle report that shows what the plan costs at the points where it strains. Every number comes from a deterministic engine; the AI handles only the language around the numbers. And it proposes, never decides — no plan becomes official until a person says so.
